# Payment Finder, recorded demo

Say what's happening in your life, in your own language, and see which Australian
government payments apply, with the official page and its date behind every line.

- **Demo:** https://vishal8shah.github.io/payment-finder/
- **Deck:** https://vishal8shah.github.io/payment-finder/deck.html
- **Code, evals and decision log:** https://github.com/vishal8shah/Services-Australia-demo

This site replays answers the pipeline generated live on 18 September 2026 for eight
example situations, in English, Vietnamese, Arabic, Chinese and Punjabi, plus two
refusals. The full version runs locally against 379 crawled public pages with Claude.

Unofficial prototype. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Services Australia. Answers
are drawn from public pages published by Services Australia, © Commonwealth of
